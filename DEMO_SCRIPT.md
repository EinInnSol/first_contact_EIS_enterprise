## 📋 Demo Flow

### Part 1: The Setup (2 minutes)

**Start with Caseworker View** to show what vendors see:

1. **Login as Caseworker** (`maria@path.org`)
   - Navigate to caseworker dashboard
   - Show client list and case management interface
   
2. **Highlight Vendor Features** (Layers 1-7):
   - "This is what service providers see - their efficiency tools"
   - Point out: Client tracking, case plans, benefit enrollment
   - **Key Point**: "Vendors love this because it saves them 2-4 hours per client"

3. **Show AI Features**:
   - Click on a client to show AI case plan generator
   - Demonstrate benefit enrollment wizard
   - **Key Point**: "AI automation makes caseworkers more effective"

### Part 2: The Reveal (3-5 minutes)

**Switch to City Admin View** - This is the "Trojan Horse" moment:

4. **Logout and Login as City Admin** (`admin@longbeach.gov`)
   - Navigate to city dashboard
   - **Pause for effect** - "Now let me show you what cities see..."

5. **Strategic Map Demonstration**:
   - Point to the Google Maps interface with vendor territories
   - Click on vendor markers to show performance metrics
   - **Key Point**: "Real-time geographic visualization of vendor performance"
   
   **What to highlight**:
   - PATH marker: "73% housing rate - excellent performer"
   - MHALA marker: "24% housing rate - underperforming"
   - Color coding shows performance at a glance

6. **Vendor Performance Comparison**:
   - Scroll to left panel showing vendor performance feed
   - Point out the performance bars
   - **Key Point**: "Cities can see which vendors deliver results and which don't"
   
   **The Comparison**:
   - PATH: 84% housing rate, trending +5%
   - MHALA: 24% housing rate (implied from demo data)
   - **Key Point**: "This data is HIDDEN from vendors - they can't see Layer 8"

7. **AI Strategic Advisor**:
   - Click "Ask Strategic Question" button
   - AI Advisor modal opens
   - Try a suggested question or ask: "Why is MHALA underperforming?"
   
   **Expected AI Response** (will vary):
   - Analysis of performance gaps
   - Data-driven insights
   - Specific recommendations
   
   **Key Point**: "Natural language interface to system intelligence - no data analyst needed"

8. **Predictive Analytics**:
   - Point to "Predictive Bottleneck" panel (orange warning)
   - Read the warning: "Shelter capacity in Sector B-4 projected to hit 100% in 12 days"
   - **Key Point**: "AI predicts problems before they happen"

9. **Export Intelligence**:
   - Click "Export Intelligence" button
   - CSV file downloads with vendor performance data
   - **Key Point**: "One-click reports for stakeholders and funders"

### Part 3: The Business Model (2 minutes)

10. **Explain the Trojan Horse Strategy**:

   **Phase 1 - Vendor Adoption**:
   - "We give vendors free AI tools that save them hours per day"
   - "They adopt voluntarily because it makes their jobs easier"

   **Phase 2 - City Revelation**:

   - **Total Market**: $115M+ ARR potential

11. **The Impact**:
   - "2 million people experiencing homelessness nationwide"
   - "$7 billion in annual funding"
   - "First Contact EIS brings accountability to a broken system"
   - "Better outcomes for clients, better value for taxpayers"

## 🎬 Demo Tips

### Do's ✅
- Start with caseworker view to build context
- Pause before revealing Layer 8 for dramatic effect
- Use specific numbers (73% vs 24%, $21K vs $78K)
- Click on map markers to show interactivity
- Ask the AI a question to show it's real
- Emphasize that vendors CAN'T see Layer 8

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't skip the caseworker view (it sets up the reveal)
- Don't apologize for any UI quirks
- Don't get lost in technical details
- Don't forget to explain the business model
- Don't rush the Layer 8 reveal - it's the money shot

### If Something Breaks 🔧
- **Map doesn't load**: Refresh the page, or say "In production this loads instantly"
- **AI is slow**: Say "The AI is analyzing real-time data across the entire system"
- **Export fails**: Say "In production this generates comprehensive PDF reports"
- **No data shows**: Fall back to explaining what WOULD be there
