# Pulse Design System: The Nervous System of Human Services

## 🏛️ Design Principles
For a product dealing with human lives, the UI must balance **Clinical Precision** (for accuracy) with **Compassionate Clarity** (for the human element).

1.  **Visual Hierarchy (The Signal vs. Noise)**: Essential data (Priority Clients, Urgent Audibles) must "Pulse" or stand out. Secondary data should fade into the background.
2.  **Glassmorphism & Depth**: Use translucency to represent the "transparency" of the platform. Layers should feel like they are floating over the "Nervous System" map.
3.  **Actionable Feedback**: Every AI recommendation should have a high-confidence visual treatment. A "60-second execution" should have a visible progress state that feels high-tech.
4.  **Spatial Awareness**: Use Google Maps not as a static image, but as a live, interactive 3D tactical floor.

## 🗺️ The "Layer 8" Geographic Strategy
The City Dashboard shouldn't just show locations. It should represent **The Flow of Need**.
- **The Heatmap**: Where are scans happening *right now*?
- **The Audit Trail**: See a line connecting a "QR Scan" at Lincoln Park to a "Housing Outcome" in a specific building.
- **The Audible Pulse**: When a cancellation happens, the map should visually "ripple" to show which nearby clients are the best candidates for the swap.
